# Report Page Plan

## Page 1 — Executive Sales Overview
- KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Units Sold, Profit Margin %, Achievement %, MoM Growth %
- Line chart: monthly Total Revenue
- Bar chart: Total Revenue by Region
- Slicers: Region, Product Category, Sales Channel, Sales Executive Name, Month-Year

## Page 2 — Regional & Product Performance
- Bar chart: Total Revenue by Region
- Bar chart: Total Profit by Product Category
- Matrix: Region, Total Revenue, Total Profit, Achievement %
- Slicers: Region, Product Category

## Page 3 — Revenue Growth Analysis
- Line chart: Total Revenue by Month-Year
- KPI cards: MoM Growth %, YoY Growth %
- Chart: Total Profit by Month-Year
- Slicers: Date, Region, Product Category, Sales Channel

## Page 4 — Sales Target & Performance
- Clustered column chart: Total Actual Sales vs Total Target Sales by Month-Year
- KPI cards: Achievement %, Target Variance
- Matrix: Region, Total Actual Sales, Total Target Sales, Achievement %
- Slicers: Region, Sales Executive Name, Month-Year
